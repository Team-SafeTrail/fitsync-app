# Survey evidence used on the FitSync landing page

Reviewed: 27 September 2026

## Safe public claims

The workbook contains 104 submitted rows.

- 84 of 104 selected more than 3 hours per week for administrative work: 40 selected 3 to 7 hours, 32 selected 7 to 14 hours, and 12 selected over 14 hours.
- 73 of 104 reported managing at least 5 learners: 31 selected 5 to 15, 32 selected 16 to 30, and 10 selected over 30.
- 101 of 104 selected a tool combination containing Zalo or Messenger.
- 93 of 104 self-described as a PT, coach, gym owner, gym manager, or directly related coaching role. The remaining submissions include ordinary gym users and one sports-science student.

Percentages shown on the website are calculated against all 104 submissions and rounded to one decimal place.

## Limitations

- The spreadsheet does not include a publication-consent field for names, contact details, photographs, or individual quotations.
- Several open-text responses repeat nearly identical wording with small punctuation or suffix changes. Those responses should not be treated as independent qualitative evidence without reviewing the survey collection process.
- The workbook does not establish a probability sample or representativeness for all Vietnamese personal trainers.
- The answers validate that respondents report a problem. They do not prove that FitSync solves it, saves a specific amount of time, or improves revenue.

For these reasons, the landing page uses aggregate counts only, describes them as a research snapshot, and does not show respondent identities or quotations.
